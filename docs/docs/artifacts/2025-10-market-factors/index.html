FACTOR KEY CONSIDERATIONS
Geopolitical Dynamics Global tensions and conflicts may disrupt supply chains, drive inflation volatility, and heighten risk premiums across asset classes
Policy Environment Shifts in monetary policy (interest rates) and fiscal policy (government spending, taxation) directly impact rate expectations and market liquidity
Inflation Dynamics Persistent cost pressures can compress corporate margins and influence central bank decisions, affecting equity valuations and bond returns
Valuation Levels Current market multiples (P/E ratios) remain elevated relative to historical averages, suggesting limited margin of safety and heightened volatility risk
Market Concentration Heavy weighting in select mega-cap companies creates concentration risk and potential for outsized market moves from sector-specific events
Asset Allocation Trends Shifting capital flows between equities, fixed income, and alternatives can create opportunities and risks across market segments
Debt Levels Elevated corporate and sovereign debt increases rate sensitivity and may constrain growth or amplify downside risk during economic downturns
Employment Trends Labor market strength influences consumer spending capacity, wage pressures, and overall economic growth trajectory
Regulatory Environment Policy changes in healthcare, financial services, energy, and technology sectors can create divergent sector performance
Structural Changes Technology innovation, demographic shifts, and climate transition may drive persistent sector rotation and performance dispersion
